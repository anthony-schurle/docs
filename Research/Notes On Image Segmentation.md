Edge Betweenness-
Let $G = (V, E)$ with $u, v \in V$ and $\sigma_{u,v}$ be the number of shortest paths between $u$ and $v$ and $\sigma({u, v}(e)$ the number of shortest paths between $u$ and $v$ containing edge $e$. Then $EB(e) = \sum_{u \in V}\sum_{v \in V} \frac{\sigma_{u, v}(e)}{\sigma_{u, v}}$.

CIEDE2000 color space.
